Preservation & Workshop Care

How to preserve crown symmetry and prevent brim warping across decades of wear.

1. Always Rest a Sombrero on Its Crown (Copa)

Setting a wide-brimmed sombrero flat on a table slowly flattens the upturned rear curve of the ala. Invert the hat so it rests on its crown or suspend it on a padded wooden peg.

2. Brushing Rabbit-Hare Felt Counterclockwise

Use a soft horsehair brush in a counterclockwise direction following the nap of the felt. For embroidered bordado hats, use a dry camel-hair brush to lift dust from metallic threads without snagging.

3. Hydrating Fine Jipijapa & Calentano Palm

In dry climates or heated interiors, natural palm fibers lose moisture and can crack along the crown pinch. Place the sombrero in a steamy bathroom for 15 minutes once a season to keep the fibers supple.

4. Drying After Rain Without Heat

If caught in a downpour, fold down the inner leather sweatband (tafilete) so air circulates inside, and let it dry in indirect shade. Never use a hairdryer, which shrinks the leather band.
